- 构建无偏估计器：$\hat{T}_{ij}^{\pi\text{-G}} = \frac{\sum_{k} w_k^{\pi} \mathbb{I}(x_k(t) \in S_i, x_k(t+\tau) \in S_j)}{\sum_{k} w_k^{\pi} \mathbb{I}(x_k(t) \in S_i)}$，其中 $w_k^{\pi} = \exp\left(-\frac{U_b(x_k(0))}{k_B T}\right)$

3) 关键结果
