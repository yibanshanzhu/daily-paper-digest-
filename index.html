- Published: 2026-09-16T11:53:30Z
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18524v1

**Abstract (truncated)**
We use replica-exchange molecular dynamics (REMD) to map the conformational free-energy landscape of the macrocyclic drug lorlatinib in explicit water and chloroform.
